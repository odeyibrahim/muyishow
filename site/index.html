The firm

In Nigeria, law does not operate in a vacuum.

A standard, not a nostalgia

Our principal trained at Fani-Kayode & Sowemimo, established in 1970 and among Nigeria’s oldest indigenous law firms. He rose to Senior Counsel at its successor practice before founding this firm.

That training gave him a discipline: rigorous preparation, institutional memory and a plain understanding of how law sits alongside government, commerce and community. We apply it daily.

A name with a history opens doors, but it is not why clients stay. The standing of this firm rests on the work behind the name: more than two decades of practice across corporate, banking, insurance, energy, property and litigation, an education at Cardiff and Essex, and a network that crosses jurisdictions.

We are a boutique by design: focused practice areas, deep Of Counsel alliances and direct access to the principal. We would rather build specialist depth than scale for its own sake.

Why Abuja

After years in Lagos, the firm moved its principal office to the Federal Capital Territory. Government permits, mining and energy licensing, federal constitutional questions and public-sector compliance all converge there. Being in Abuja means being at the table, not travelling to it.

The same position lets us act as a dependable Nigerian anchor for inbound capital, joint ventures and cross-border transactions, working alongside foreign counsel and advisers rather than apart from them.

Our Abeokuta office serves growing businesses and entrepreneurs in Ogun State, where proximity to Lagos, a fast-moving property market and significant mineral resources are producing increasingly sophisticated commercial activity.

The Principal

LL.B. (Cardiff), P.G.C.E., M.A. (Essex)
